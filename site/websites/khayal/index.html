02 — Studio Brief

A studio practice drawing the line from first sketch to final keying.

Khayal is not a contractor with a moodboard or a designer with a subcontractor list. We are the studio that holds the project from the concept board to the day the client first sits in the finished room. Architecture, interiors, fabrication oversight — handled under one roof, with the same hand on the pencil and the file.

"We measure a room by what it asks of you when you stop talking."

We build slowly on purpose. Every detail — the radius on a stone edge, the depth of a reveal, the warmth of a fixture — is decided on paper, mocked on site, then specified before a single trade is briefed. The result is a room that holds together at close range and at distance.

04 — Execution System

How we build a room.

A drawn process from first call to handed-back keys. Five disciplined stages, supervised in-house. No subcontracted creative direction. No reactive site decisions.

Project Axis · A — A'
/ 01

Consultation & brief

~ 3 days
A1 · 00.00
  • Site survey — dimensions, light, structural notes
  • Brief gathering — lifestyle, rhythm, non-negotiables
  • Budget banding — material tier, scope
  • Studio fit — both sides decide
/ 02

Concept & plan

~ 4 days
A2 · 04.30
  • Concept direction — palette, mood, references
  • Plan studies — walls, daylight, circulation
  • Style fit — Modern, Classic, Scandi or Minimal
  • Sign-off — drawn-board review
/ 03

Drawings & 3D

~ 7 days
A3 · 08.60
  • Shop drawings — every joint, every reveal
  • 3D visualisation — photoreal verification
  • Materials library — physical samples on table
  • Specification set — schedule of finishes
/ 04

Build & supervise

~ 3 weeks
A4 · 14.40
  • Trades briefed to our drawings, not to a moodboard
  • Weekly site walks with photo record
  • Mock-up sign-off for joinery, stone, plaster
  • QA register — every detail tracked to closure
/ 05

Handover & keying

~ 3 days
A5 · 20.00
  • Punch list closed before handover
  • Final styling — linens, books, the kitchen pots
  • Walk-through with manuals and warranties
