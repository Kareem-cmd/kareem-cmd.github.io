// SECTION 00 MANIFESTO · WHY WE EXIST

We exist to make the invisible visible.

The internet is the greatest illusion we have ever built — a planetary machine that hides its workings behind glass and gloss. Roman Dahy exists to take it apart, slowly and cinematically, until the magic reveals itself as something better: understanding.

01

Curious, never condescending

We open the hood with you, not for you. The viewer is a peer, never a student.

02

Cinematic, not corporate

Every frame should feel composed. Every word, deliberate. No filler. No noise.

03

Bilingual by nature

EN & AR. Latin script & Naskh. Two minds, one signal. Always inspiring.

04

Tech is human

Every protocol has a story. Every packet has a journey. We honor both.

// SECTION 04 VISUAL MOTIFS · SYMBOLIC LIBRARY

Six motifs. One world.

Each motif carries a specific meaning. Use one per composition — never all at once.

04.A

Chrome Brain

Thinking. Open mind. The act of breaking something down. Use as hero motif for deep-dives.

04.B

Chrome Sphere

Reflection. Insight. The moment understanding solidifies. Use as supporting hero or accent.

04.C

Insight Bulb

The aha moment. Golden — use sparingly. Only for genuine breakthroughs. Never decoration.

04.D

Neural Nodes

The network. The internet. The pattern of connection. Use as pattern, background, or accent.

04.E

Synthwave Grid

The foundation. The plane on which ideas live. Use as floor in hero and atmospheric scenes.

04.F

Star Dust

Atmosphere. Depth. The sense of cosmic space. Use as ambient layer behind everything.

// SECTION 05 COMPOSITION · LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

Layered. Quiet. Intentional.

Every composition follows the same three-layer logic. Respect the negative space.

LAYER 03

Background

Void black + film grain + star particles. Always.

LAYER 02

Motif

One 3D object — brain, sphere, or bulb. Centered. Photoreal.

Let's Breakdown
LAYER 01

Typography

Flat, sharp, sans + script. No 3D treatment on type.

PRINCIPLE 01

Negative space wins

Empty is not empty — it is breath. Reserve at least 40% of every composition for stillness.

PRINCIPLE 02

One hero per frame

One headline. One image. One mood. If two elements compete, cut one.

PRINCIPLE 03

Asymmetry breathes

Center is for sacred moments. Default to off-center, weighted, intentional.

PRINCIPLE 04

Grain is non-negotiable

Every surface gets 12% film grain. It is what makes the digital feel analog.
