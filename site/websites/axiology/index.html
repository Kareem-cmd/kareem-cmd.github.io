01

Harvested at dawn.

Boswellia trees in the highlands of Oman. We wait for the right season, then nick the bark by hand and let the resin bleed slowly into the sun.

02

Distilled, never extracted.

No solvents. No shortcuts. Steam carries the oil out of the resin, the way it's been done for two thousand years — only the volume is smaller.

03

Blended in Cairo.

A small workshop in Heliopolis. Three of us, two scales, one quiet music playlist. Every batch is small enough to remember by name.

04

Worn on your skin.

Three drops on a fingertip, pressed in upward strokes. Then breathe. The skin meets the oil — and a thousand years of ritual continues.
